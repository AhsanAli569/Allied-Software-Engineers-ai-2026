About Allied Software Engineers

Allied Software Engineers (Private) Limited is a Pakistani software consultancy
company, incorporated under the Companies Act, 2017 (XIX of 2017) with the Securities
and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP). It is a company limited by shares.

Principal line of business:
Allied Software Engineers provides consultancy services in software development,
including project management, IT requirements management, system analysis and
design, programming, software testing, software quality assurance, outsourcing of IT
and telecommunication services, IT consultancy, and IT process development services,
to both international and domestic markets. The company also carries out research
and development in the field of Computer Sciences.

The company's Memorandum of Association specifically excludes it from engaging in
regulated or prohibited activities such as banking, non-banking finance, insurance,
stock brokerage, forex trading, multi-level marketing (MLM), pyramid or Ponzi
schemes, or lottery business.
